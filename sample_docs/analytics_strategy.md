# Analytics Strategy for Senior Analysts

## Forecasting

Quarterly forecasts use the forecasting notebook template, which combines a seasonal baseline with known events such as launches and holidays. Publish forecasts with an interval, not a single number, and record the assumptions next to the forecast so they can be checked afterwards.

## Stakeholder Partnerships

Each senior analyst partners with one or two business teams. Meet the team lead every two weeks, keep a shared list of open questions, and push back on requests that do not name a decision. The goal is to change decisions, not to produce charts.

## Experimentation Reviews

Senior analysts review experiment readouts before they are shared. Check that the sample-ratio test passed, that the primary metric was chosen before launch, and that the readout reports confidence intervals rather than only p-values.

## Mentoring

Senior analysts mentor at least one junior analyst, review their queries weekly and help them present their first analysis to stakeholders.
