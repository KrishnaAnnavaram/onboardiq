# Dashboards and Metric Definitions

## Metric Layer

Official metrics such as weekly active users, conversion rate and net revenue are defined once in the metric layer. Dashboards must read metrics from the metric layer rather than re-implementing the formula in the BI tool. If a metric you need is missing, propose it in the analytics guild channel.

## Dashboard Standards

Every dashboard has an owner, a description of its audience, and a last-reviewed date. Put the most important number in the top left, label units on every axis, and show comparison periods. Dashboards not viewed for 90 days are archived.

## Ad-hoc Requests

Log every ad-hoc request in the analytics request board with the business question and the decision it supports. Requests that would take more than two days are prioritised weekly by the analytics lead.

## Checking Your Numbers

Before sharing a number, reconcile it against the official dashboard for the same period. Differences above 1 percent must be explained in the message that shares the number.
